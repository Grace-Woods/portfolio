# Other
Outside of the educational and professional setting, I thoroughly enjoy being a part of my local community.

### Philanthropy
As a member of the Gamma Chi chapter of Delta Delta Delta, I work with my fellow members to fundraise for St. Jude Children's Research Hospital. Despite the small size of our chapter, each year we are able to raise thousands of dollars, and most recently, we were able to surpass our previous fall quarter fundraising by over six times. Since joining in fall 2023, I have personally raised over $1,500 in support of the families receiving care at St. Jude.

### Lacrosse
In addition to working as a student manager for the university's Division 1 Women's Lacrosse team, I play for the club team, competing at the WCLA Club Division 2 level. I have played lacrosse almost my entire life, so being able to continue playing and being involved in the sport at the collegiate level, has been very fulfilling.

### Animal lover
I have always been a huge dog lover, having three of my own at home. I miss them all the time while being in Denver for school, so I recently started walking dogs in the area through the app Rover. This has been an amazing way for me to still be around dogs, while being able to give me insight on what managing a small business could look like. This adventure has allowed me to build a lot of connections with people in area, while teaching me a lot about responsibility.
