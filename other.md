# Other
Outside of the educational and professional setting, I thoroughly enjoy being a part of my local community.

As a member of the Gamma Chi chapter of Delta Delta Delta, I work with my fellow members to fundraise for St. Jude Children's Research Hospital. Despite the small size of our chapter, each year we are able to raise thousands of dollars, and since joining in fall 2023, I have personally raised over $1,500 in support of the families of St. Jude. Most recently, we managed to surpass our previous fall quarter fundraising by six times.
